# Market Dashboard Working Agreements

These instructions apply across the repository. Preserve user-owned changes and follow the nearest nested `AGENTS.md` or `AGENTS.override.md` if one is added later.

## Product intent

- Preserve the existing workflow. Put new information at the point where it supports a decision instead of creating a new dashboard area by default.
- Prefer the smallest complete change. Do not turn a cell, label, or popup request into a broader redesign.
- In Analyst Sources, show the shared broker connection once as `IB Gateway`, with its captured connection status. Keep holdings, balances/account exposure, and open-order acquisition checks internal; a failed data request alone must never imply a disconnected Gateway. Preserve independent market-data, research, and model checks and saved captures' original evidence.
- Do not add trading, sizing, allocation, recommendation, or order controls unless the user explicitly requests them.
- When the user corrects the same behavior twice, encode the preference here or in a regression test.
- Use Hermes for requested recurring automation unless the user explicitly asks for Codex scheduling.
- The Analyst is the only active Hermes agent. Quant Analyst, Quant Developer, Security Analyst, their worker schedules, legacy director UI, and worker snapshot bridge are retired; do not restore their heartbeat expectations or Inbox warnings. Keep Research delivery status in Sources/Systems instead of creating generic delivery Inbox messages. Preserve actual security controls and publication discrepancy evidence.

## Market data integrity

- Preserve complete research publications when individual source values are inconsistent. Import the valid content and independent fields, retain the exact questionable values with their source, and surface the discrepancy in the Analyst Inbox. Withhold only invalid fields from calculations or executable decisions; never silently correct the publisher, discard the rest of the report, or treat successful import as resolution of the discrepancy.
- Never present mock, seeded, inferred, model-only, portfolio-derived, cost-basis-derived, or disconnected cached values as executable market data.
- Keep quote status separate from strategy or signal status. A valid bullish or bearish signal may still have an unavailable quote.
- Distribute independently published directional Trade signals through the shared security research projection to every applicable dashboard, Portfolio, and Analyst surface. Preserve the original source, publication date, instrument identity, and explicit Trade evidence; retain the latest dated publication when subsequent reports omit that security. Show Trade separately from Trend. Never relabel a Trend, Buy Trade/Sell Trade range bound, portfolio listing, or source position as directional Trade, and never make an old research reading a current executable signal.
- During an open market, show a verified live quote as `LIVE`; otherwise show a verified delayed quote as `DELAYED` with its observation time, or `UNAVAILABLE`.
- Keep verified official session closes identified in underlying provenance. In the informational overview and daily market ribbon, present them with the same orange/red clickable `D` as delayed observations instead of a `LAST CLOSE` label; show the original observation/session time when the D is clicked. This display convention never makes a session close a live or executable quote.
- For executable trading data, treat disconnected providers, failed refreshes, stale flags, cache-only responses, seeded rows, and mock fixtures as unavailable. Do not preserve old executable rows after the provider reports offline or error.
- The main dashboard Asset Performance, Dollar, and Volatility tables (including G5RV) are informational overviews. Keep each asset's last verified provider observation through partial responses, failed refreshes, provider disconnects, aging, page reloads, and API restarts. Show retained readings with the shared `D` marker and their original provider/observation time; never refresh the timestamp or label them live. Replace them when a newer verified reading arrives. Show dashes only when no verified observation exists. Retained overview readings have no age-based deletion deadline. A missing or rejected row must never overwrite that series' verified observation, and persistence must save the merged per-series result. Cover this contract with regression tests; do not reintroduce expiry blanking under live-data cleanup. This does not permit fabricated data or retained values in execution, sizing, or order controls.
- Populate every supported Asset Performance timeframe from verified history for the exact displayed instrument, including S&P 500 and Russell 2000. Quote and history providers may differ for the same instrument: an IBKR cash-index quote must not clear native Yahoo index returns. Retain each verified timeframe and its original history provenance through quote-only and partial refreshes, page reloads, and API restarts; a missing timeframe never erases an existing verified reading. Keep different sovereign benchmarks and observation frequencies separate, and never substitute ETF or futures returns for cash-index returns.
- The six daily market-strip panels (breadth, leadership, winners and losers, outliers, themes, and factors), including their expanded readings, follow the same informational retention contract. Retain each last verified reading through expiry, partial or rejected responses, failed refreshes, disconnects, page reloads, and API restarts; use `D` with the original provider, observation time, and session date. Persist the merged result without an age-based deletion deadline. Preserve each breadth denominator with its own observation, rankings as complete comparable groups, and factor pairs with both original legs; never calculate new rankings or spreads by mixing sessions. A fresh missing field must not erase a verified reading. Never replace retained observations with mock, seeded, inferred, portfolio-derived, or unverified cache-only data.
- Disable or hide any control that requires an executable quote when only delayed, last-close, or unavailable data exists.
- Carry provider, observation time, market-session date, and freshness metadata from the source to the UI. If freshness or provenance cannot be established, omit the value.
- Never manufacture bid, ask, mid, depth, edge, probability, or price from complementary sides, averages, position marks, cost basis, models, or stale caches.

- Setup rule (user-specified): Long when Trend and Tick are both Bullish and price is in the bottom third of the Risk Range; Short when both are Bearish and price is in the top third. Trade blocks a setup only when it is published and points the other way; missing Trade never blocks. Price outside the range is no setup. Compare a range only with its own instrument (e.g. Nasdaq-100 shows the Composite `COMPQ` range positioned with the Composite price, labelled), and read HedgAI Treasury price-ETF Ticks (SHY, UST) as inverse to yields. ETH has no Tick; never borrow Bitcoin's. Sizing and line split (user-specified 2026-11-10; parameters in the catalog `sizing` block, code in `src/services/predictionSizing.cjs`): sizes are % of the Kalshi account (cash plus open positions), 10% max per position. A setup in an above/below ladder market (metals, crypto, oil, indices; not rates or CPI) starts at 4% and earns up to 6 more points: Risk Range location 2 (edge of the range full, third boundary none), published Trade agreeing 1, 20-session Z-score stretched against the side 1 (2 sd full), US Dollar Trend through the 30D correlation 1 (half when |corr| < 0.5 or the dollar is Neutral), volatility 0.5 (none when the gauge is in its High band or up more than 10% in a week), volume signal 0.5. A missing reading scores half and is named; rounded to 0.5%. The position is split across the selected event's lines by edge against our own odds: each liquid line (both sides quoted, spread ≤ 5¢) takes its YES mid through the backtest calibration curve (`src/constants/kalshiCalibrationCurve.json`), shifted toward the setup by up to 0.25σ at a 10% position (none at 4%) and widened 1.1× when volatility is expanding. Short buys NO and Long buys YES at that side's own quoted ask; edge = our odds − ask − fee; lines with edge under 1 point get nothing, the rest share the position by Kelly weight `edge / (1 − ask)`, a share under 0.25% of the account is dropped, and a stake is cut to the size resting at the ask with the remainder shown as unspent. There is no price floor on lines. Tickets render as an `analyst-sizing` block under the section's market table (market, side, size of 10%, the inputs that cost points; then Line | From centre | Buy | Ask | Kalshi odds | Our odds | Edge | % of account | $ · contracts; then unspent, Kalshi's ladder 1σ beside the vol index's implied move, and quote time). Without a readable Kalshi balance the split shows shares of the account only. The first split shown for each event on a market day is saved as a paper ticket (`PredictionMarketPerformanceLedger`, account `paper`) and scored at settlement; `GET /api/prediction-markets/paper-ledger` reports results by market, side, price and size input, and the report carries one `Paper tickets:` line above Sources. The 0.25σ tilt is an assumption the ledger tests (`artifacts/kalshi-line-backtest/replay-sizing.cjs` replays it over history). Nothing places orders.

- Risk Range watch (user-specified): every held security is placed in its own published Risk Range, read from the central security record. Risk ranges reset every trading session, so only a range dated the current session counts anywhere: the daily Risk Ranges sheet when it prints the security, or an ETF Pro edition dated that same session. An earlier session's range shows nothing and raises nothing, and each session's first reading counts as entering its zone. A proxy or unmatched range is never shown for another security. Zones use the Predictive Markets thirds: 33% and below is the bottom, 66% and above the top. Inbox range cards draw the full Predictive Markets bar (low, high, third levels, price, % in range), centered under the card's copy, with no separate Risk Range text row. Positions rows show a one-line meter with % of range, expanded rows chart closes against the range printed for each session, and securities with no published range read `—`. Inbox Notifications, blue, one condition → action line, raised once per zone entry each session: worded tersely as `Top 1/3 RR (73%)` and `Bottom 1/3 RR (14%)`, with plain actions. A price past either end reads as that end's third with its true percentage (`Top 1/3 RR (127%)`, `Bottom 1/3 RR (-20%)`) and only that reading is blue, as a range top above the all-time high is; the Signal label, arrow and action stay standard and never coined shorthand. Short at or below the low end, or Long at or above the high end → reduce position; Short in the top third or Long in the bottom third → add to position; Short above the high end or Long below the low end → moving against your position. A verified position that grows short at or below the low end, or long at or above the high end, reads `→ should be MIN size` and replaces that day's zone card. You hold 3.18%.`), never naming the person. Every Inbox notification with no Action row leads with its Signal in the same bold first line an Action uses. A zone change needs the price clear of the line by 2% of the range's width. Unverified holdings raise nothing and never read as a position change.
