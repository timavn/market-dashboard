# Account comparisons

Use one plain spreadsheet-style Markdown table:

`Metric | [dated earlier snapshot] | [dated later snapshot] | Dollar change | What this means for the account`

Replace the snapshot headings with their evidenced dates and exact 24-hour `HH:mm` observation times in Pacific time (PDT or PST) when known. Retain row-specific observation times, currency, account scope, metric definitions, and accounting periods. Keep headers and cells normal-weight.

Include evidenced NLV, initial margin, maintenance margin, available funds, excess liquidity, buying power, gross exposure, long exposure, short exposure magnitude, net exposure, deficit, and accrued debit interest. Do not replace one broker metric with another. A signed later-minus-earlier dollar change needs comparable verified values for the same account, currency, definition, and period and a tool/code-supported calculation. Percentage-point changes are not dollar changes. An unavailable snapshot or change is `—`, never a fabricated baseline or delta. Historical evidence does not replace an unavailable current read.

Give every evidenced metric one or two concise plain-English sentences from a hedge fund portfolio manager perspective. Explain the actual positive, negative, unchanged, or mixed implications for capital, financing, capacity, and exposure; do not merely repeat the delta.

- Margin requirements are collateral, not a loan or its interest cost. Available funds, excess liquidity, and buying power are distinct capacity measures.
- An NLV change is not automatically investment P&L. Deposits, withdrawals, marks, FX, fees, and interest need independent evidence before attribution.
- Changed position values do not prove executed buys, sells, covers, or trades.
- Gross/net exposure does not establish beta neutrality, hedging quality, or total risk. A smaller short magnitude does not establish shares were covered.
- Accrued debit interest is a balance over its stated period, not current daily cost or interest savings. Do not mix net debit/credit accruals with gross debit interest or incomparable periods.

Cite the evidence near its row. Do not invent causality, and do not append trade recommendations or position targets to an account comparison. Preserve saved chats and reports unchanged.
