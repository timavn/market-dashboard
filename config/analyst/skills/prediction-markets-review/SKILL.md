# Prediction markets

A saved Predictive Markets Review owns its captured research and numeric comparisons. Interpret it without recreating the report.

Current reviews are a setup board: per Kalshi market, Tick (HedgAI), Trade, Trend, price, Risk Range and position in range, and Setup. Long = Trend and Tick both Bullish with price in the bottom third of the range; Short = both Bearish in the top third; a published opposite Trade blocks, a missing Trade does not. Line selection and sizing are not defined yet; do not invent them. Structure compares each Risk Range with its earlier publications over the last 10 sessions: today's move (HL/LL for the low end, HH/LH for the high end), the consecutive run ending today (an unchanged range neither adds nor ends a run), and ATH when the range top is above the instrument's all-time high. The ideal Long reads a higher low, a higher high and ATH; Structure grades a setup and never changes the Long/Short rule. Volume is the latest completed session of each market's ETF (GLD, SLV, CPER, USO, BNO, IBIT, ETHA, SPY, QQQ, SHY, IEF, TLT; UUP for the Dollar) against the prior session and its 15-, 30- and 90-calendar-day averages (about Hedgeye's 10-day, 1-month and 3-month). DV is volume below its 30-day average and AV above it; a pullback in a Bullish Trend, or a bounce in a Bearish one, on DV is when Hedgeye adds, and on AV it is a warning. Volume grades a setup too. There is no live event-contract quote tool: answer from the saved review's captured board, use `get_portfolio_context` for Tim's held contracts, and say when a current native quote is unavailable rather than estimating one. Portfolio impact requires `get_portfolio_context` in the same turn, and capacity requires `get_balance_context` and the relevant `get_process_context` rules. A review narrative follows the exact market scope captured in that review.

Keep the exact venue, underlying, event, reference month, comparator, expiration, resolver, and settlement reference. Use the review's captured comparisons. Native contract odds are market-implied from that side's directly verified quote and payout; they are not a calibrated forecast. Label gross payout/risk before fees. Never derive a NO quote from YES, average prices into a probability, invent expected value, or derive a probability from Risk Range location. The immediately adjacent threshold must actually be adjacent for the same event, settlement, comparator, and venue.

Trade and Trend need independent dated publications. A missing Trade is unknown, not Neutral or disagreement. Keep Tick and its source quality separate. CPI uses its exact release-matched forecast lower/base/upper values, not a spot price or Risk Range. Other assets require a comparable source instrument and price basis; do not transfer SPX ranges to ES, Composite ranges to NDX, or Bitcoin-only research to Ethereum. Retain quoted-level versus last-close selection meanings.

## Format

For the optional review narrative, add only material interpretation under the relevant asset and venue; normally at most 300 words. Do not repeat numeric rows, empty asset sections, report titles, diagnostic lists, missing-Trade commentary, or a closing process-status summary. If the existing narrative request calls for no output when nothing additional is supported, follow it. Keep sources in compact asset footers.

For a targeted comparison, answer first and use a small table with the exact outcome (`>`, `<`, `≥`, or `≤`), native side/ask, market-implied odds, gross payoff before fees, and expiration only when those fields help. Keep underlying Risk Range reward/risk separate from contract payoff/risk. Unsupported analytical values are `—`. Recommend a qualifying line or process action only with fresh native data and code-computed capacity; do not invent a setup or place orders.
