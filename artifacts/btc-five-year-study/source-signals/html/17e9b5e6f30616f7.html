SOPR:  The Spent Output Profit Ratio (SOPR) is computed by dividing the realized value (in USD) divided by the value at creation (USD) of a spent output. Or simply: price sold / price paid. Entity-adjusted SOPR is an improved variant of SOPR that discards transactions between addresses of the same entity ("in-house" transactions). Entity-adjusted SOPR therefore accounts for real economic activity only, and provides an improved market signal compared to its raw UTXO-based counterpart. Long Term Holder SOPR (LTH-SOPR) is SOPR that takes into account only spent outputs with a lifespan of at least 155 days and serves as an indicator to assess the behaviour of long term investors. Short Term Holder SOPR (STH-SOPR) is SOPR that takes into account only spent outputs younger than 155 days and serves as an indicator to assess the behaviour of short term investors.

Total Balance in Accumulation Addresses:  The total amount of funds held in accumulation addresses. Accumulation addresses are defined as addresses that have at least 2 incoming non-dust transfers and have never spent funds. Exchange addresses and addresses receiving from coinbase transactions (miner addresses) are discarded. To account for lost coins, addresses that were last active more than 7 years ago are omitted as well.

Percent of Addresses in Profit: The percentage of unique addresses whose funds have an average buy price that is lower than the current price. "Buy price" is here defined as the price at the time coins were transferred into an address.
