SOPR: The Spent Output Profit Ratio (SOPR) is computed by dividing the realized value (in USD) divided by the value at creation (USD) of a spent output. Or simply: price sold / price paid.  Adjusted SOPR is SOPR ignoring all outputs with a lifespan of less than 1 hour.  Long Term Holder SOPR (LTH-SOPR) is SOPR that takes into account only spent outputs with a lifespan of at least 155 days and serves as an indicator to assess the behaviour of long term investors.
