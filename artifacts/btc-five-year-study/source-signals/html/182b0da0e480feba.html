On Chain Cost Basis By Holder:  Realised Price reflects the aggregate price when each coin was last spent on-chain. Using Short- and Long-Term Holder cohorts, we can calculate the realised price to reflect the aggregate cost basis for each group. The LTH-STH Cost Basis Ratio is then calculated as the ratio between LTH and STH realised price. This metric:  Uptrend when STHs are realising losses at a greater rate compared to LTHs (e.g. bear market accumulation). Downtrend when LTHs spend coins and transfer them to STHs (e.g. bull market distribution). Trade > 1.0 when LTH cost basis is higher than STH (e.g. late stage bear market capitulations)

 

Supply Liquidity: The liquidity of an entity is defined as the ratio of cumulative outflows and cumulative inflows over the entity's lifespan. An entity is considered to be illiquid / liquid / highly liquid if its liquidity L is ≲ 0.25 / 0.25 ≲ L ≲ 0.75 / 0.75 ≲ L, respectively.
