How to access your Bitcoin Trend Tracker subscription

1
Log-in to Hedgeye.com

You'll need the User Name and Password you created when you signed up. Forgot your password? Click here to reset it.

2
Access Bitcoin Trend Tracker

Select Bitcoin Trend Tracker on Hedgeye.com using the menu drop down in the top left-hand corner of your screen.

3
Enjoy your Bitcoin Trend Tracker subscription

Bitcoin Trend Tracker is automatically emailed to you before the market opens each morning. The latest version of Bitcoin Trend Tracker (and all back-dated issues) appear in chronological order on our website. Continue scrolling down to access the archive.

Your subscription provides access to our highly sought after, proprietary buy and sell risk ranges are specifically designed to help you make better sales at the top end of the bitcoin risk range and purchases at the low end. You’ll also receive a variety of price, volume, volatility, sentiment and market data that our Macro team monitors to inform our fundamental views on Bitcoin.
