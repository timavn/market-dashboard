Futures Perpetual Funding Rate: The average funding rate (in %) set by exchanges for perpetual futures contracts. When the rate is positive, long positions periodically pay short positions. Conversely, when the rate is negative, short positions periodically pay long positions.

SOPR:  The Spent Output Profit Ratio (SOPR) is computed by dividing the realized value (in USD) divided by the value at creation (USD) of a spent output. Or simply: price sold / price paid. 

Long Term Holder SOPR (LTH-SOPR) is SOPR that takes into account only spent outputs with a lifespan of at least 155 days and serves as an indicator to assess the behaviour of long term investors. Essentially, what was the avg price the coins last moved at.

LTH Spent Price: The ratio of Market Price to LTH SOPR. Extreme peaks and troughs in this metric often occur around local market tops and bottoms, often an indicator of strategic profit taking (high profit multiples), or panic selling (lower profit multiples), respectively.
