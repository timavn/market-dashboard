This is a design preview for a local portfolio dashboard, not live trading data. Flat white/off-white application canvas, dark navy text, muted slate labels, very thin pale blue-gray rules, royal blue portfolio marks, restrained green/red only for favorable/unfavorable ranks. Graphic heading "+EV factor alignment". Category navigation: "Asset classes", "Equity sectors" (selected), "Equity styles", "Fixed income". These are separate comparison families; NEVER place Gold or bond factors in the equity-sector chart. All categories are accessible by these tabs.

ALL ELEVEN sectors must be present including zero exposure sectors.
On the five Best rows draw a broad pale-gray reference bar BEHIND a thinner solid royal-blue portfolio bar. Gray guide bars decrease monotonically with worksheet rank, with lengths proportional to 5,4,3,2,1. Blue bars encode the stated percentages on a consistent portfolio scale. Make Energy's 12% blue bar clearly much longer than its rank-guide background and Tech's 8% clearly shorter than the largest guide. No numeric percentage is printed on a gray benchmark bar. Materials has a dash for the guide. Bottom five nonpreferred rows have only blue actual bars with no gray target; Utilities has a clearly visible blue 7% bar and a restrained amber mismatch indicator. Do not represent an absence of a guide as a mandated zero allocation.
At chart top a tiny legend: "Blue: portfolio longs" and "Gray: relative rank guide". Small helper sentence "Guide scaled for comparison; not target weights." A very light separator before Materials and before the unfavorable rows is sufficient, not separate cards.
At bottom of the chart a single plain text insight, no alert box: "Energy leads the book. Tech leads Q2. Utilities deserves a closer look."
No spider charts, donuts, pies, oversized KPI cards or extra sidebars. Excellent visual alignment and easy scanning are vital.
