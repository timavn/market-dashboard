Use the previous combination grid for the underlying research idea only.
Use the ACTUAL MAIN DASHBOARD as the decisive visual-style reference: dense ruled tables, restrained typography, square geometry, narrow gray headers, normal-sized numerical readings, virtually no vertical cell padding.
Do NOT preserve the previous concept's large fonts, tall controls, spacious cells, or boxed summary card.

Output a sharp straight-on desktop UI mockup, landscape approximately 1536x768, tightly framed around one centered rectangular Bitcoin Research window.
