# Expanded Bitcoin Research matrix concepts

Created with built-in image_gen using the user's selected B screenshot.

Both concepts remove the complete top title/banner row and place the USD rule in a dropdown beside the other study controls. No product code changed; these are static concept images.

1. Full matrix: 15 rows (three regimes by five entry zones) ×12 columns (three USD directions by four volatility states), 180 visible combinations. An outcome selector makes all percentages consistently up-close or down-close frequencies.
2. Directional matrix: 15 rows (five zones by three USD directions) ×8 columns (bullish/up and bearish/down, each with four volatility states), 120 visible combinations. Green intensity encodes up-close frequency; red intensity encodes down-close frequency.

Cell percentages, counts and confidence intervals are illustrative design examples, not computed or verified research. Hours and distinct-day counts remain visible; small-sample examples are marked and left unshaded. The minimum-sample concept does not establish statistical reliability.

Selected-cell detail retains a baseline, difference, count and confidence interval. Actual implementation must compare on matching factor coverage, retain original observation times, define the historical BTC volatility metric and states, and keep unknown combinations unavailable.
