## Concept 1

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished high-fidelity desktop screenshot concept for redesigning an existing personal financial research window called "Bitcoin Research". This is an image design exploration, not a live product, and not actual backtest data.
Overall design: exceptionally straightforward, concise and calm. LIGHT MODE ONLY. White canvas, light gray section headings, fine gray borders, square corners, charcoal sans-serif text, normal 400 weight values and labels, bold small headings only. Green for positive percentage changes and bullish, red for negative changes and bearish, gray for neutral/chop. No blue, rounded pills, gradients, illustrations, gauges, stars, hero charts, trading controls, buy buttons, decorative icons, branding, or giant typography.
Canvas: landscape 1536x1024 or similar. Show one compact centered rectangular workspace window on a very softly blurred light-gray market dashboard background. Main window fits comfortably without scrolling, and occupies about 90% width and 75% height. Text must be highly legible, crisp and consistently aligned. Outer frame square corner. Close X upper-right. A slim centered square-corner bottom dock beneath the window contains "Research", "Portfolio", "Prediction Markets", "Market Events", "The Analyst", "Systems".
Title bar "Bitcoin Research" left. At right, clearly visible "DESIGN CONCEPT · EXAMPLE VALUES". This label is essential because every percentage and sample count in the mockup is illustrative.
One top study setup row has "5-year study" with "Coverage ›" nearby, "Hourly ▾", "Bullish ▾", "Bottom third ▾", and "Every qualifying hour ▾". Bullish green. These are compact square controls with gray borders. Do not claim five full years of factor coverage.
Only two primary additional filters: "U.S. dollar" and "BTC volatility". U.S. dollar choices "Off", "Down", "Up", with Down selected. Inline rule details "≥10 bp · 30-session correlation ≤−0.50", plus a subtle "Edit rule" affordance. Volatility choices "All", "Investable", "Chop", "Uninvestable", with Investable selected. Investable green, Chop gray, Uninvestable red. No numeric volatility thresholds. Filters are combined using AND. These are historical research conditions, no current-state or live badges.
All values are illustrative: same eligible-history comparison, one-hour open-to-close BTC spot outcome. Baseline 58.0% closed up, 1,000 hours / 180 days. Dollar only 63.0% closed up, 400 hours / 85 days, change +5.0 pp. Volatility only 61.0% closed up, 500 hours / 100 days, change +3.0 pp. Both 66.0% closed up, 200 hours / 40 days, change +8.0 pp vs baseline or +3.0 pp vs dollar.
At bottom preserve supporting existing information as compact collapsed disclosure links, not a large table: "All entry zones ›", "Year by year ›", "Volume & Quad ›", "Method & sources ›", "Export". No scrolling.
A concise footer line "Illustrative values · Percentages measure closing direction." reinforces the mockup label.
Do not use the terms win rate, guaranteed, optimal, trade now, or validated. No invented live quotes. No decorative financial chart.
